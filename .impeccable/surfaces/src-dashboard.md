Scope: the extension dashboard (home list, editor, settings). Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: Italian second-hand sellers, private and reseller, at a desk with the marketplace tabs open; write a listing once, send it to five sites, know where every item is live.
Must stay: name Splisto, every function and every piece of copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: every listing is an object seated in its own recess on a dark velvet tray, the five marketplaces engraved brass tags along its rail. It refuses the category default: near-black SaaS panels with one neon accent and glowing card edges.

OWN-WORLD: bottle-green velvet ground with a fine nap texture, recesses as inset wells darker than the ground, hairline brass rules, bone-ivory text. Brass is solid only on what can be pressed; everywhere else it is engraved. Site state is a mark in a fixed cell: filled verdigris online, hollow not sent, vermilion struck error, amber half when it needs you. Headings in an engraved roman (Marcellus); all UI, labels and numbers in one workhorse sans (Albert Sans) with tabular figures.

STORY: the seller sees the tray, every item in its slot at the same photo scale, and reads at a glance which of the five tags each item carries; one brass button starts a new piece.

FIRST VIEWPORT: brass nameplate SPLISTO top left, brass "Nuovo annuncio" top right; the five site plaques in one rail beneath with their state marks and counts; then the tray of long listing slots.

FORM: own grounded list position 5 (jeweler's velvet display tray), seed key 7d1b135d. Raises: fixed-cell state marks (timetable), brass only on pressables (warm app), tray recomposes in whole slots with sold pieces greyed (roster), same photo scale and ground for every item (botanical folio). Signature interaction: a slot's photo lifts out of its recess on hover and focus, the shadow deepening beneath it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
